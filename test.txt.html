Project: Rezoning Paradox
Time Series Python Public Data

Quantifying the Impact of Blanket Rezoning

Business Problem: Did deregulation actually increase housing supply?

Calgary passed a controversial citywide rezoning policy in 2024 to combat housing shortages. Using public construction data, I conducted an independent audit of the policy's immediate effects.

  • Outcome: Discovered a paradoxical 16% decrease in construction starts immediately following the policy, contradicting official forecasts.
  • Methodology: Comparative market analysis adjusting for seasonal baselines.
  • Impact: Provided stakeholders with the first quantitative evidence of "market friction" caused by policy transitions.
