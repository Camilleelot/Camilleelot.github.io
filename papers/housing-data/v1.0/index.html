About This Dataset

Curator: Camille Moreau

Version: 1.0

Published: November 15, 2025

Data Coverage: November 2023 - March 2025

Description

This repository contains curated housing market data for the Calgary Census Metropolitan Area, sourced from the Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation (CMHC). The dataset was assembled to support policy analysis of Calgary's housing market dynamics, particularly the August 2024 blanket rezoning intervention.

All data is sourced from CMHC's official surveys and is provided under Statistics Canada's Open License, which permits use for research, analysis, and visualization purposes.

Datasets Included

1. Housing Starts by Dwelling Type

Directory: data/housing_starts/

Description: Monthly housing construction starts for Calgary CMA, disaggregated by dwelling type (single-detached, semi-detached, row houses, apartments).

Format: CSV

Temporal Coverage: November 2023 - March 2025 (17 months)

Use Cases:

  • Interrupted time series analysis of policy interventions
  • Compositional analysis of housing construction
  • Density trend analysis

2. Raw CMHC Survey Data

Directory: data/raw_data/

Description: Unprocessed CMHC survey data as downloaded from official sources. Preserved for reproducibility and verification.

Format: Various (CSV, Excel, as provided by CMHC)

Note: Raw data may require cleaning and processing before analysis.

3. Rental Market Indicators

Directory: data/rental_data/

Description: Rental market data for Calgary CMA, including vacancy rates, average rents, and market indicators.

Format: CSV

Source: CMHC Rental Market Survey
