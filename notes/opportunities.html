What the GitHub actually argues§ 01

The public site is stronger than a typical 2026 BA resume, and weaker than a five-year specialist posting. That gap is the whole strategy: apply where the work is the credential, skip where years and French and a master’s are the credential.

  • Subject matter: Calgary housing supply after blanket rezoning; transit cuts versus voting; nonprofit retention; fleet ops for a 250-person NGO; Russian-language FPV doctrine for Canadian Arctic policy.
  • Methods: interrupted time series, spatial joins, STPD borrowed from geology, cohort/cost modeling, BM25-vs-dense eval for compliance retrieval, primary-source OSINT.
  • Shipped products: Streamlit turnover tool, Google Apps Script FleetTracker in production, this site (hand-written HTML/D3), Imagine Canada 103-standard accreditation support.
  • Languages: English and Russian fluent. French is “working on it,” which is not CMHC CBC bilingual.
  • Calendar: B.A. Political Science (Psychology minor), Athabasca, 2026, finished while working. Operations Administrator, Closer to Home, May–Dec 2025. Independent research ongoing.

Do not lead applications with “open to research, analysis, and writing roles.” Lead with the one artifact that would make that hiring manager’s week easier.

Which artifact to put in the letter§ 05

§ 01 Transit / vote
City Intergovernmental, Issue Strategist, any “you will write for elected officials” seat. One sentence: you matched 225 ridings to journey-to-work data, then used the Green Line cut-and-restore as the test the national correlation could not run.
§ 02 Rezoning
City Planning & Development Analyst, future CMHC analyst, anyone who lives in CMHC starts. One sentence: after blanket rezoning, starts ran 16% below trend; composition shifted toward multi-family and still did not close the gap.
§ 03 STPD
Distress Centre, any NGO people-analytics seat, APS competitions that want “complex assessments.” One sentence: you lifted a geology turning-point method, used involuntary turnover as a control, and the Wellness Days break was 3× prior changes.
§ 04 Turnover tool
Distress Centre and similar. Demo link in the first paragraph. Do not lead with Streamlit; lead with the board-report sentence.
§ 05 FleetTracker
Ops / analyst hybrids, Imagine Canada-adjacent nonprofits. Proof you ship on Workspace constraints, not that you want a fleet job.
§ 06 Ingest Gap
CSE language analyst, CSIS HUMINT (as the language/analysis asset, not as a claim that you have already been an intelligence officer). One sentence: you read Russian FPV training channels in the original and mapped a doctrine gap against Canada’s published no-fly-in-icing posture.
attest · flexrecap
Secondary. Attest is the accreditation-retrieval story if a nonprofit mentions standards. Flexrecap is a methods footnote (claims checked against match data), not a League-of-Legends anecdote, unless the interviewer already laughed.
