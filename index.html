The Rezoning Paradox: A Complete Policy Cycle

Research Question: Did deregulation actually increase housing supply?

I tracked Calgary's blanket rezoning policy from proposal through implementation. First, I authored a community-based research report analyzing how financialization transforms housing into investment assets—and recommended aggressive rezoning among five policy solutions. When the city implemented rezoning in 2024, I returned to measure what actually happened.

  • Pre-Implementation: "A Manufactured Crisis" — policy analysis examining financialization, NIMBYism, and the theoretical case for rezoning.
  • Post-Implementation: ITS analysis revealed a paradoxical 16% decrease in construction starts (Cohen's d = -1.18), contradicting official forecasts.
  • Impact: Research engaged University of Calgary faculty and generated civic discourse on evidence-based policy evaluation.
