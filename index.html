Quantitative Tools

NGO Turnover Analysis Tool — Interactive dashboard for nonprofit organizations to understand employee retention patterns.

Built to address a fundamental challenge in the social services sector: turnover rates r ∈ [40%, 65%] are known, but the underlying dynamics—when people leave, why they leave, and what it costs—remain obscured.

Implements case-based tenure analysis (categorizing terminations ∀e ∈ T₀ by tenure t), cohort retention tracking, Sankey flow diagrams, and cost modeling. Upload Excel data → receive actionable insights.

Features: Six case types (0-3mo, 3-12mo, 1-3yr, etc.), voluntary/involuntary breakdowns, department flows, predictive risk flags. Designed for HR managers who've never touched code.

Systems & Automation

FleetTracker for NGOs — Open-source fleet incident management system deployed in production at a 250+ employee behavioral health nonprofit.

Transforms vehicle incident reporting from email chaos and PDF filing into an automated workflow. Google Form → Apps Script → Organized Drive folders + Email notifications + Maintenance tracking. Zero infrastructure cost.

Built on Google Workspace (which nonprofits often get for free). Handles incident logging, photo archiving, insurance lookup, service date tracking, and stakeholder notification—all automatically.

Impact: ~8 hrs/week saved, 100% documentation rate, same-day response times. Designed for organizations with 10-30 vehicles. Free to deploy, step-by-step guide included.

The etymology of this project is based on my name, Camille, and the mythical castle of King Arthur, Camelot, as well as a play on words; this is a place to store 'a lot'.
