About

Policy researcher finishing my Political Science degree at Athabasca University. I transform complex public civic data into clear narratives; currently working on my analysis of zoning changes in Calgary, and documenting how zoning changes reshape communities through both quantitative analysis.

My work looks at questions that occupy space in everyone's mind; How do regulatory changes ripple through markets? What stories do city documents tell? When the rubber meets the road and policy changes are enacted, what actually changes?

This site serves as a portfolio host, but also as a playground, a chance to use data science skills, with real data and real world implications.

Data Analysis Tools

NGO Turnover Analysis Tool - An interactive dashboard for nonprofit organizations to understand employee retention patterns.

Built to address a fundamental challenge in the social services sector: turnover rates r ∈ [40%, 65%] are known, but the underlying dynamics—when people leave, why they leave, and what it costs—remain obscured.

The tool implements case-based tenure analysis (categorizing terminations ∀e ∈ T₀ by tenure t), cohort retention tracking, Sankey flow diagrams, and cost modeling. Upload Excel data → receive actionable insights.

Features: Six case types (0-3mo, 3-12mo, 1-3yr, etc.), voluntary/involuntary breakdowns, department flows, predictive risk flags. Designed for HR managers who've never touched code.

The entymology of this project is based on my name, Camille and the mythical castle of King Arthur, Camelot, as well as a play on words; this is a place to store 'a lot'.
