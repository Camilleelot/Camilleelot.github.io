FleetTracker - Production Google Form Questions
================================================

This document reflects the ACTUAL production form deployed at CTH.
All question titles must EXACTLY match for the script to work correctly.

CRITICAL: If you rename ANY form question, you MUST update the corresponding
constant in FleetTracker.gs (see the CONFIGURATION section).

FORM HEADER
-----------

Title: Vehicle Incident Reporting

Description/Instructions:
"
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS AT THE SCENE

Stop and Assess Safety: Check on passengers and others involved for injuries and move to a safe place.

Call 911 IMMEDIATELY IF:
• Anyone is injured or requires medical attention.
• There is a fire or risk of fire.
• The collision is blocking live traffic lanes and creating a major hazard.
• You suspect another driver is impaired by alcohol or drugs.

Contact Your On-Duty Manager: This is a MANDATORY step. Before proceeding further, call the On-Duty Manager to report the situation and receive immediate guidance.

Determine if Police Are Required at the Scene: you must wait for police to arrive if the collision involves ANY of the following:
• An injury or fatality.
• An impaired driver.
• An out-of-province vehicle.
• An unidentified vehicle (e.g., hit and run).
• Significant vehicle or property damage (appearing to be over $5,000 combined).
• The collision involves a government vehicle.

If the situation does not meet these criteria but damage still exceeds $5,000, you must file a report at a police station within 24 hours.

• DO be polite and cooperative with others involved.
• DO NOT admit fault, apologize for the collision, or assign blame to others.
• DO NOT promise payment or agree to forget about the damage.
• State only the facts of what happened when speaking to the police.
"

VEHICLE INFORMATION SECTION
----------------------------

Question 9: Vehicle In Question (Year/Make/Model/License Plate)
Type: Dropdown
Required: Yes (if company vehicle)
Options: (Populate from your Master Vehicle List)
  - This will be the most tedious part, list out all vehicles like this, same format
  - 2024 Honda ABC-1234
  - [Add one entry per vehicle in your fleet]

CRITICAL FORMAT REQUIREMENT:
- Format MUST be: Year Make Model PLATE
- License plate MUST be the last word (after the last space)
- License plate MUST match EXACTLY what's in Master List and VEHICLE_FOLDER_MAP
- Example: "2024 Honda ABC-1234" (script extracts "ABC-1234")

Notes: The script extracts the license plate by taking the last space-separated token

CONDITIONAL LOGIC SUMMARY
--------------------------

The form uses skip logic to show/hide sections based on responses:

1. "Is this a Company vehicle?"
   - Yes → Show vehicle dropdown (Q9) → Continue to incident details
   - No → Skip to injury tracking (Q28)

2. "Were other drivers or vehicles involved?"
   - Yes → Show other driver info section (Q12-19)
   - No → Skip to police involvement (Q20)

3. "Did the police attend the scene?"
   - Yes → Show police information (Q21-24)
   - No → Skip to witnesses (Q25)

4. "Did anyone else witness the incident?"
   - Yes → Show witness information (Q26)
   - No → Skip to photos (Q27)

This keeps the form streamlined - drivers only see relevant questions.

DEPLOYMENT NOTES
----------------

1. Question titles are CASE-SENSITIVE and SPACE-SENSITIVE
   - "Date of Incident" ≠ "Date Of Incident" ≠ "Date of incident"
   - Match the constants EXACTLY

2. The vehicle dropdown is tedious but critical
   - List EVERY vehicle in your fleet
   - Use EXACT format: Year Make Model PLATE
   - Double-check license plates match your Master List

3. File uploads require special setup
   - Form creates a folder automatically for uploads
   - Note the folder ID for UPLOAD_FOLDER_ID in the script

4. Conditional logic is managed in Google Forms
   - Edit each multiple choice question → Click three dots → "Go to section based on answer"
   - Set up skip logic as documented above
