FleetTracker - Suggested Google Form Questions
===============================================

IMPORTANT: Question titles MUST exactly match the field name constants in FleetTracker.gs
If you change a question title, update the corresponding constant in the script.

---

Question 1: Date of Incident
Type: Date
Required: Yes
Notes: This will be the primary date used for incident tracking

---

Question 2: Time of Incident (if known)
Type: Short answer
Required: No
Placeholder text: "e.g., 2:30 PM"
Notes: Allows drivers to specify time if known; not required since it may be uncertain

---

Question 3: What home does the driver fall under?
Type: Dropdown
Required: Yes
Options: (Customize for your organization)
  - Main Office
  - North Campus
  - South Campus
  - East Facility
  - West Facility
  - Other
Notes: Helps route incidents to the appropriate program manager

---

Question 4: Full Legal Name of the Driver
Type: Short answer
Required: Yes
Validation: None (free text to handle various name formats)
Notes: Used for incident folder naming and accountability

---

Question 5: Vehicle In Question (Year/Make/Model/License Plate)
Type: Dropdown
Required: Yes
Options: (Populate from your Master Vehicle List)
  - 2020 Honda Civic ABC-1234
  - 2019 Toyota Camry XYZ-5678
  - 2021 Ford Transit DEF-9012
  - 2018 Nissan Sentra GHI-3456

CRITICAL FORMAT REQUIREMENT:
- Format MUST be: Year Make Model PLATE
- License plate MUST be the last word (after the last space)
- License plate MUST match EXACTLY what's in your Master List and VEHICLE_FOLDER_MAP
- Example: "2020 Honda Civic ABC-1234" (the script extracts "ABC-1234")

Notes: The script uses the license plate to look up vehicle data. DO NOT allow "Other" or manual entry for this field.

---

Question 6: Vehicle Damage/Photos & Scene Diagram
Type: File upload
Required: No (but strongly encouraged)
Settings:
  - Allow multiple files: Yes
  - Maximum number of files: 10
  - Maximum file size: 10 MB per file
  - File types: Image files (jpg, png), PDFs
  - Upload location: Your "Form Upload Temp" folder

Notes:
1. Go to question settings → "Go to folder" to verify upload location
2. Copy the folder ID from the URL - this is your UPLOAD_FOLDER_ID for the script
3. Emphasize to drivers: TAKE PHOTOS IMMEDIATELY at the scene (damage, other vehicles, context)

---

Question 7: Brief Description of What Happened
Type: Paragraph
Required: Yes
Placeholder text: "Describe the incident in detail: What happened? Where were you driving? Were other vehicles involved? What was damaged?"
Validation: Minimum character count (optional): 50 characters

Notes: This is the narrative section - encourage detail for insurance claims

---

Additional Optional Questions (Customize as needed):
----------------------------------------------------

Question: Were there any injuries?
Type: Multiple choice
Options: Yes / No / Unsure
Notes: Helps flag incidents requiring immediate follow-up

Question: Was a police report filed?
Type: Multiple choice
Options: Yes / No
If Yes, ask: "Police Report Number" (Short answer)

Question: Were other vehicles involved?
Type: Multiple choice
Options: Yes / No
If Yes, ask: "Other Driver's Insurance Info" (Paragraph)

Question: Estimated Damage Severity
Type: Multiple choice
Options:
  - Minor (cosmetic damage, no mechanical issues)
  - Moderate (functional damage, vehicle drivable)
  - Severe (vehicle not drivable, towing required)

---

Form Settings Recommendations:
-------------------------------

1. Collect email addresses: Optional
   (Useful if you want to send a confirmation to the driver)

2. Response receipts: "Always"
   (Drivers get a copy of their submission)

3. Restrict to [Your Organization] domain: Optional
   (Depends on whether contractors/volunteers need access)

4. Allow response editing: No
   (Prevents tampering after submission; use a new submission for corrections)

5. Confirmation message:
   "Thank you for submitting your incident report. The operations team has been notified automatically and will follow up within 24 hours. If this is an emergency, call [EMERGENCY NUMBER] immediately."
