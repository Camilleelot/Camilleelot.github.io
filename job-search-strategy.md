# Job-Search Strategy: People Analytics / Total Rewards

A working playbook for landing an analytics role at a mainstream / "normie-tech"
employer with strong trans-inclusive benefits. The benefit is the *filter*; the
role is the analytical work I already do. This is a personal planning doc, not a
public portfolio page.

---

## 1. The goal, stated plainly

Land a stable, well-benefited analytics role whose employer funds comprehensive
gender-affirming care. Prioritize speed and fit over prestige — but aim
deliberately at the higher-value People/Comp analytics lane, not just generic
"data analyst," because my HR-data + causal-inference combination is rare there.

---

## 2. Target role families (priority order)

1. **People Analytics / HR Data Analyst** — the sweet spot. Pays above generic
   DA, leans on HR experience few applicants have, and is the on-ramp to Total
   Rewards and (later) People Data Scientist. Comp seen in live 2026 Canada-remote
   postings: $121–189K (Wealthsimple, Grafana, Motive tier).
2. **Total Rewards / Compensation Analyst** — comp benchmarking, pay equity,
   benefits cost modeling. $78–115K+ (Drax, Bora tier). Often gated on 5 yrs +
   a designation → treat as *stretch reaches* I back into via people analytics.
3. **Data Analyst (SQL/BI)** — the floor / backup. SQL + Excel + Power BI.
   Entry ~$58K / junior ~$84K / mid ~$105K.
4. **Broaden the net (same résumé):** Compensation Analyst, HR Data Analyst,
   Workforce Planning Analyst, Pay Equity Analyst, HRIS / Reporting Analyst,
   People Operations Analyst.

## 6. Credential roadmap (surgical, not a grind)

Reality check: I already have the highest-weighted assets — real HR/ops work
experience, a finishing degree, and a public portfolio. Certificates are an ATS
filter, not the decider. Stacking rule: one comprehensive + one tool-specific,
max. Order by ROI:

**Tier 1 — cheap, fast, do-now (passes ATS):**
- **Microsoft PL-300 (Power BI Data Analyst Associate)** — ~$165, one exam.
  Validates a skill I already use; exact-match ATS keyword in Microsoft-shop
  enterprises. *Highest-ROI first move.*

**Tier 2 — differentiators for the higher-paying lane (optional):**
- **dbt Analytics Engineering Certification** — thin supply vs. demand; one of
  the few certs that "moves offer letters." Pairs with learning dbt + a cloud
  warehouse (BigQuery free tier).
- **AIHR People Analytics Certificate** (~$1,125) — HR-specific, practical.
  Let an employer fund it rather than paying out of pocket.

**Tier 3 — gold-standard but LATER / employer-funded (do NOT grind pre-offer):**
- **CCP (Certified Compensation Professional, WorldatWork)** — *the* total-rewards
  credential (listed as "strong asset" on comp postings), but 8 exams / ~$7,812 /
  ~2-yr journey. Land the role first, then have them pay. Only write "working
  toward CCP" once actually enrolled.
- **CPHR Alberta** — Canadian HR designation; good long-game, needs experience.
- **IAPP AIGP** — for the AI-governance north-star, much later.
- **Master's (MPP / MS Analytics)** — not required for these roles; slow and
  expensive. Skip for now.

**Skill-ups (free/cheap, high-ROI):** SQL window functions & CTEs fluency; dbt +
BigQuery free tier; one tidy predictive model (logistic regression attrition) to
make the "predictive analytics" claim concrete.
